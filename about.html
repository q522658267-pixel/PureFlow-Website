Company Profile

Foshan PureFlow Health Technology Co., Ltd. is a professional manufacturer of air purification equipment, dedicated to creating clean and healthy environments for homes, businesses, and industries worldwide. We specialize in pet air purifiers, leveraging advanced biological enzyme technology to effectively eliminate pet odors, hair, and allergens.

With over a decade of experience in air quality solutions, our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility combines cutting-edge technology with rigorous quality control to deliver products that exceed international standards. Our team of engineers and researchers continuously works on developing advanced filtration technologies to address growing concerns about indoor air quality.

We have established a comprehensive R&D system and hold numerous patents and certifications, reflecting our commitment to innovation and excellence. Our products are exported to over 30 countries across Europe, North America, and Southeast Asia, earning recognition from customers worldwide for their superior performance and reliability.
